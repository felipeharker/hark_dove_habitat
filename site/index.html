A modular cage accessory system: 3D-printed mounts and accessories for a wire corner cage

Rev A | 20 Oct. 2026

Abstract

Standardised, 3D-printable mounts and accessories for a wire corner cage. Every accessory attaches through the same interface, so any accessory fits on any mount and can be moved or swapped with nothing more than a hex key.

Base Cage

Standard Interface

A printed mount clamps to the cage wires once. Accessories then slide into it, lock with a pin, and come out again in seconds. Each accessory is the same stack of layers, from the cage wire outward.

MP-1 wire mount
Clamps onto the 5/8″ wire grid. Kit: MP-1, a BP-1 backer and an LK-1 pin, fixed with 4 zip ties or 2 × M4.
KA-1 key adapter
A dovetail key that slides down into the mount and is locked by LK-1.
Swappable head
Fastens to KA-1 with 4 × M3 screws into heat-set inserts.
Function parts
The dowel, cup, deck, board or link that the head carries.

In the split drawings, colour shows what each part does: teal for the mount, blue for the key adapter, orange for structure, green for a perch, red for feeding, purple for a platform, yellow for shelter, pink for a toy, tan for wood to buy or cut, and grey for hardware to buy.

Design Principles

Built to the grid
The mount’s four wire grooves sit on the cage’s 15.875 mm pitch; NB-1’s two keys are exactly six wires apart.
Quick swaps
Pull the pin, lift the accessory out, drop another in.
Printable as is
Every part is laid out in print orientation on a 220 mm bed, with no supports.

MP-1 Wire Mount Kit ShowHide

Mount12 mm reach82 × 84 mm W × H1 mount

MP-1 is a 60 × 84 × 11 mm printed plate. Its back carries four half-round grooves on the cage’s 5/8″ (15.875 mm) wire pitch, so it seats over four vertical wires and can’t slide sideways. The front has a dovetail channel that is open at the top and closed by a 6 mm stop at the bottom. A KA-1 key drops in from above and rests on the stop. The LK-1 pin then passes through a cross-bore 8 mm below the top edge, through both channel walls and the key tongue, so a bird can’t lift the accessory out.

Features

  • 4 × Ø3.2 mm wire grooves at 15.875 mm (5/8″) pitch
  • Zip-tie slots above and below the centre line, sized for 4.8 mm ties
  • 2 × M4 clearance holes (Ø4.4 mm, Ø8.5 mm counterbore) 20 mm apart in the channel floor
  • Ø4.3 mm LK-1 cross-bore, 8 mm below the top edge
  • Dovetail channel: 25 mm mouth, 30.4 mm base, 6 mm deep

Options

Zip-tie fixing
4 × 4.8 mm ties through the slots and around the wires. Quickest; nothing is needed outside the cage.
Bolted fixing
BP-1 backer outside the cage, 2 × M4 screws from the channel side into M4 nuts held in BP-1’s hex pockets. Both plates have matching wire grooves, so the wires are clamped between them. Use it for heavy accessories or positions a bird works at.

Compatibility

Mount fixing
Zip ties (4 per mount) or bolted with a BP-1 backer and 2 × M4. The choice is per mount and doesn’t affect what can be attached.
Interchangeable parts
A PH-1 head takes a 3/4″ dowel of any length or the PP-1 grip perch. A PH-2 pair takes one dowel across a corner. SB-1 takes any 1/2″ board.
Placement
Horizontal position steps in whole 5/8″ wire pitches. Height is free along the vertical wires, between cross-bars. Two-mount accessories need both mounts level.

Assembly

Print
P1 and P2 twice, P3–P8 once. PETG, 4 walls, 40% gyroid, no supports.
Set inserts
4 × M3 heat-set inserts into the Ø4.0 mm holes on each KA-1.
Fit heads
Each head to a KA-1 with 4 × M3. NB-1 takes two keys and 8 screws.
Add function parts
Dowels, PP-1, cup ring, deck, shelf board, NB-1 dowel, quick link.
Fix mounts
MP-1 over four vertical wires, channel up; 4 zip ties, or M4 through to a BP-1 backer.
Hang
Lower each key into its mount until it rests on the bottom stop.
Lock
Push LK-1 through the side bore. To swap, pull the pin and lift the accessory out.

Care

  • Check zip ties and LK-1 pins when cleaning; replace any tie a bird has chewed.
  • Cup, deck and lid lift off for cleaning without removing the mount.
  • Use untreated hardwood and stainless hardware where birds can reach.

Open Items

  • Screw lengths (M3 head screws, M4 mount screws) and heat-set insert length aren’t set in the model. Insert holes in KA-1 are Ø4.0 mm.
  • Wood screws for SB-1 (Ø4.2 mm holes) and the perch retaining screws (Ø3.5 mm holes) still need a size.
  • The KA-1 tongue is ≈0.2 mm taller than the MP-1 channel is deep, so a seated key stands ≈0.2 mm proud of the mount face. Confirm this preload is intended.
  • Check each mount position against the panel’s horizontal cross-bars before fixing.
